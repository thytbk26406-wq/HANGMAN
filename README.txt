HANGMAN LEVEL 3 - HARD

Cach chay:
1. Cai dat PyQt6 neu may chua co: pip install PyQt6
2. Mo thu muc nay trong VS Code hoac PyCharm.
3. Chay file main.py.

Cac tep:
- main.py: Khoi dong game, xu ly giao dien, tinh diem va mo bang diem cao nhat.
- gd_hangman.py / gd_hangman.ui: Giao dien duoc giu nguyen tu Level 1.
- xuli.py: Xu ly logic doan tu, mang va diem.
- word_data.py: Danh sach 10 tu vung va goi y cua Level 3.
- thuvien_max.py: Luu diem theo Easy, Medium, Hard.
- maxscoregiaodien.py / maxscoregiaodien.ui: Man hinh diem cao nhat dung chung.
- diem_cao.json: Tep du lieu diem cao nhat.

Level 3 su dung bo khung va co che tinh diem cua Level 1. Diem duoc cong 1 khi doan dung mot tu. Ten level duoc dat la Hard va diem duoc luu vao muc Hard.
